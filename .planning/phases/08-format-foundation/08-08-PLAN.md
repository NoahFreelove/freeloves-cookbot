        INSERT INTO RecipeTags (RecipeId, Name)
        SELECT r.Id, TRIM(json_each.value)

    - `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` defends against duplicate-case-identical entries already in TagsJson (e.g. `["Vegan","Vegan"]` produces one row).
