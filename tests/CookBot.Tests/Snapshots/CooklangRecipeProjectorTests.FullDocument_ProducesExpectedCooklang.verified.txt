>> servings: 8
>> prep time: 20 min
>> cook time: 60 min
>> source: Family Cookbook, Grandma Rose, https://example.com/recipes/pound-cake
-- Equipment: stand mixer
-- Equipment: bundt pan

== Cream Butter ==
Beat butter and sugar together until light and fluffy.
-- 72°F
-- pale and fluffy
== Combine ==
Add eggs one at a time, then vanilla extract.
Fold in all-purpose flour gradually. ~mix{2%min}
== Bake ==
Pour into prepared bundt pan and bake. ~{60%min}
-- 350°F
-- toothpick comes out clean

@all-purpose flour{2%cups}
@butter{1%cup}
@sugar{1 1/2%cups}
@eggs{4}
@vanilla extract{1/2%tsp}

-- Substitution (butter): dairy-free option
